Was reminded today that it is my two-year anniversary at MongoDB! How do I feel after two years in Developer Education?

I love my job. I love going to work every day, having the opportunity to learn new things constantly, and hopefully make life better for the developers who use Realm Database and MongoDB App Services. I have loved using these things myself in personal projects to better understand the folks we write code examples and documentation for - and I also just genuinely think they're great tools and *like* using them.

I love working with smart people who are generous with their time and knowledge. I love feeling supported and challenged by my team and my work. This is such a happy role for me that unites my interest in app development and my background in writing.

I am very grateful to be where I am and looking forward to year three!
