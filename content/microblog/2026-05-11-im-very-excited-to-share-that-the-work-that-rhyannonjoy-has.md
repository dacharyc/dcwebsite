I’m very excited to share that the work that [@rhyannonjoy](https://github.com/rhyannonjoy) has been doing to study web retrieval across agent platforms has made it back to the Agent-Friendly Documentation Spec in the form of a new Platforms page! 🎉

Rhyannon breaks down for us three distinct axes of the web retrieval pipeline across platforms:

- Retrieval
- Truncation
- Summarization

The picture is a lot more complicated than I realized when I started my research with Claude Code a few months ago. Expect bigger updates to the spec soon as we grapple with how to integrate the cross-platform findings into something docs teams can take action on.
