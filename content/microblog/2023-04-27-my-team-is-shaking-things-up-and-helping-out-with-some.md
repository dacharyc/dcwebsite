My team is shaking things up and helping out with some wider docs projects, which gives me a chance to help out with the Realm Kotlin SDK docs for a bit.

This is my first time really touching Kotlin, and I probably would have been intimidated to try a few languages ago. But after branching out from Swift to help out with C++, Flutter, and now Kotlin docs, plus building tools in TypeScript and Python, I’m not intimidated to touch new languages anymore. And I’ve learned that if you know a set of APIs really well, it’s not that difficult to figure out how to use them in another language.

Still loving a job that constantly lets me learn new things and grow my skills. Grateful all the time for the recruiter who reached out to me in 2020 about a job that united my tech writing background with a chance to grow development skills.
