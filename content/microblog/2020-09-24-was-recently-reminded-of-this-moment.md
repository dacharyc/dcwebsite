Was recently reminded of this moment: the culmination of a four-month motorcycle trip spanning nearly 18,000 miles across 13 countries, from Boston to Ushuaia.

I learned a lot of important lessons on that trip that have made me the person I am today. But one of the most valuable was the knowledge that whatever obstacles are thrown in my path, I can figure out a way over, around, or through them and keep moving forward.

That lesson has gotten me through so many hard days and hard times, both at work and in my personal life.
