Exciting announcement time: after 14 years as a contractor, I've finally hung up my freelance hat to go to work full-time for Tugboat !!!

As of today, I'm officially starting as "Technical Content Strategist" for Tugboat; a role that's part marketing, and part technical documentarian. I'm so excited to be joining the talented team alongside Ben Chavet, James Sansbury, Matt Westgate (he-him), and a TBA additional member of our crew ;)

Many thanks to the Tugboat crew for making me feel like a member of the team in the past year while I've been contracting for them, even before making the transition to full employee. I'm so delighted to make it official!

And thanks to the Lullabot leadership team, our parent company, for taking the leap of faith to devote resources to this awesome tool. I've really enjoyed working with the Lullabot crew, and am so glad to be joining the team.

This is a deeply weird time, and none of us can predict what the future holds, but I am filled with optimism to be joining this team and seeing what we can accomplish together.
