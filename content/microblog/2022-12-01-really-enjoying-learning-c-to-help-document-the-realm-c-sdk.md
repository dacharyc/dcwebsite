Really enjoying learning C++ to help document the Realm C++ SDK our engineers are rolling out. I dabbled in C a little bit when I was going through some Computer Science fundamental courses and it’s fun to dip my toes in #cpp. And of course, it’s great to have a lead like [@cbush](https://github.com/cbush) who knows the language well and is happy to explain things and help me over some newb debugging hurdles.

I’m so happy to have a job I enjoy this much; one that lets me learn new things all the time  and work with smart and supportive teammates who strive to help one another be successful. #MongoDB talks about how it wants to be an inflection point for people’s careers, and it has certainly been one for me. So grateful to the recruiter who reached out to me in late 2020 about joining the Developer Education team, and to the lead who advocated for having me on the team even though I didn’t have a traditional development background. This place is great and my job is awesome 😁
