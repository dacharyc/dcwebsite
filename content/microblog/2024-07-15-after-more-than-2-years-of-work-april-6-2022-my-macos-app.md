After more than 2 years of work (April 6, 2022!), my macOS app, PR Focus, is finally ready for public beta!

If you are a #TechnicalWriter who works with engineers who occasionally forget to mention when something needs a documentation update, or...

If you are a #Developer or #DocsAsCode technical writer who makes or reviews PRs, or is assigned work in GitHub...

You might find PR Focus a useful tool to help you keep track of PRs across multiple repositories - all in a single dashboard.

I've just finished adding more info and some App Preview videos to the website: https://prfocus.app/

I have been using it daily at work for over a year in its current incarnation, have had a group of colleagues using it to provide feedback, and am finally satisfied enough with the level of fit and finish to share it with others. I have gotten *so much* value from it, it has kept me learning and working through the queue of bugs and grown to the point that it might be quite useful to others.

If you're interested in joining the public beta, you can join the TestFlight beta group here: https://testflight.apple.com/join/PmdjrF6U
