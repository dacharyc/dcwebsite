This was a rough week for people in tech. One of my best friends lost his job this morning. My husband lost his job earlier this week. Thousands of other people are in the same position right now. The timing going into the holiday season is pretty awful.

My thoughts are with everyone who finds themselves suddenly unemployed right now. I hope you all land somewhere that values you more than the places you just departed.
