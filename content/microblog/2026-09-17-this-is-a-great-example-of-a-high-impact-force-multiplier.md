This is a great example of a high-impact force multiplier when it comes to using AI for docs.

It’s also near-and-dear to my agent experience philosophy; agents churn and cannot recover when error information is lacking. I think better error information is one of the most universal agent experience improvements a product can make; all agents will hit errors from time to time, and making those errors recoverable is AX in action.

I’ll even go on record as saying that spending time remediating/improving error information is *more* important than spending time on Agent Skills. Not all customers will use your skills, but all customers will hit errors.
