One of the writers in the org just reported an issue with my tooling, which prompted me to look at our running GitHub Actions and I saw a sea of red.

Ran this little `ghok` command line utility (GitHub, are you OK?) and I can see there are active incidents affecting GitHub Actions.

Good reminder that having little utilities like this that meet you where you are make all the difference. I never have to look up the GitHub status page, or bookmark it, and wade through reports - just type a few characters into one of the many terminals I have open at any given time and see output like this.

If you are a technical writer who sometimes uses the command line for things, I cannot recommend enough starting to customize the utilities available to you to make your life a little easier. Before long, you will build up a library of useful things and wonder how you ever did anything without them.
