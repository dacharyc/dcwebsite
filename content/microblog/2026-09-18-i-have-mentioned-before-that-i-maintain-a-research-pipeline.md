I have mentioned before that I maintain a research pipeline to help me stay on top of AI-related research.

Sometimes that produces gems like this one in my daily report: “Theme ‘Emergent Unsanctioned Autonomy in Frontier Agent Evaluations’ transitioned from emerging to active” 🥲🫠

In case I missed it in recent news, the narrative summary of research activity names the OpenAI compaction summary jailbreak incident. (I didn’t miss it.) I enjoy that the summary included it alongside new research related to over claiming, agents inconsistently completing tasks, and misreporting when they do. 😂

If you wondered why I maintain a pragmatic view of what the tools are capable of, it’s because I have a daily view of what the industry is doing - both good and bad.
