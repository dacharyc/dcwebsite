Hubby had an idea for an app that he shared with me on Friday night. I knew we could achieve it with a minimal amount of work using MongoDB’s Atlas Functions, Device Sync, and Twilio. We started working on it on Saturday morning, and by Sunday night we had a working app that could receive text messages via Twilio, insert them into Atlas, and sync them with a SwiftUI #iOS client using Device Sync. I put up the app on TestFlight under 48 hours after we started working on it.

This right here is why #MongoDB is an amazing platform for #developers to build on. It gives us fantastic tools to build *fast* and takes care of so much hard stuff so we can just build cool things. Psyched to take this from TestFlight beta to published app.

Will I get it done in 2022? Let’s find out!
