My team has been working across a lot of different repositories lately, so I've set up my PR tracking app, PR Focus, on my new work computer.

It flagged ~5 PRs that needed attention across the 11 repositories my team works in, and I was able to take action on those PRs.

It has also made my team's work more visible in the very active monorepo we work in daily - the monorepo averages ~260 open PRs at any given time, but I only care about the ones my team is making.

Good reminder that having tools that support the way you work makes work a lot easier!
