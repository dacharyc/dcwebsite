An interviewee asked me yesterday if I was still at MongoDB in 5 to 7 years, how would I feel about that? Would I be happy about it?

Obviously things can change, but at the moment, yes, I am planning to be at MongoDB that long and anticipate still being happy to be here.

I have freelanced for most of my career because it gives me flexibility, and I like being free to do the work that appeals to me. Freelancing gave me the opportunity to always be learning new things, and to pivot if I got bored.

But I love my work at MongoDB, and the freedom my manager gives me to pursue the projects that are interesting to me. I feel supported and valued by my teammates. I am surrounded by smart people who help me grow. The work itself is satisfying.

I can honestly say that MongoDB is the first place in my career that I feel I’ll be happy and want to stay for a very long time.

Thank you to the interviewee for helping me realize that! And may we all be as happy with our work.
