Another shoutout to [@rhyannonjoy](https://github.com/rhyannonjoy) for contributing more known platform limits to the Agent Friendly Documentation Spec!

This time, Rhyannon dug into Cursor. Reading their writeup of the methodology reveals a lot of assumptions we're probably all making about how agents fetch data: https://rhyannonjoy.github.io/agent-ecosystem-testing/docs/anysphere-cursor/methodology

(And spoiler: we're probably wrong about at least some of them!)

And the friction log reveals how complicated it can be to test these things.

Super appreciative of their work here. Rhyannon has become a substantial contributor to the spec at this point with all the deep dives on platform-specific findings, and if you have been wondering how to test things like this, their site is a great learning resource.

Really grateful to have them along on this research journey!
