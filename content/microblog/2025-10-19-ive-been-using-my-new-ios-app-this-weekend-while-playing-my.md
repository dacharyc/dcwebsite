I’ve been using my new iOS app this weekend while playing my new-to-me game.

I wanted a few small improvements - custom searchable implementations for Characters to find people who like certain gifts (whatever I happen to have on me) and Recipes to see what recipes I can make with certain ingredients (whatever I happen to have on me).

Pushed the new build to TestFlight and have been using it today while playing and boy oh boy is this a nice feature 😁

I really do love building apps that do exactly what I want them to do in the way I want them to do it. 💖
