Well, thanks to [@caseyrfsmith](https://github.com/caseyrfsmith)'s urging, I've been trying out Claude Code for the last few weeks... I've just canceled my Augment Code account.

Augment was good, but after they switched to credits-based pricing, I was basically using my entire monthly allotment in two days.

I was afraid that using Claude directly, I would hit my rate limit for each 5-hour session or weekly limit and be out of luck. Compared to Augment, though, having to wait for a 5-hour session or even a weekly session to reset is better than only getting to use my tool 2 days per month.

So long, Augment Code. 🫡
