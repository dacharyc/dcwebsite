Do your docs fill any customer support obligations? Do you have SLAs or customer agreements that require docs to be available to your customers?

Well, here's a Thursday afternoon thought exercise for you... with more and more software development being outsourced to agents, when do "agent-friendly docs" become an SLA issue? When does it start showing up in customer agreements?

I think we're very early in growing awareness of this being it's own *thing* to measure, but I'm really curious what y'all think about this.

(Also, good reminder you can use `npx afdocs check https://your.docs.domain.example.com to check whether your docs are agent friendly, and start thinking about what you need to do to get there.)
