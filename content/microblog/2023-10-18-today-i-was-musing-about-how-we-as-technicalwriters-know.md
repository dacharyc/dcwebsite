Today I was musing about how we as #TechnicalWriters know when to update documentation. Are there processes or strategies you use to find out about changes to the product? Do you rely on product or engineering to tell you about upcoming changes? Are you embedded on teams where you're involved in planning and can decide for yourself when to update docs?

I had a few thoughts based on how I do things - including a tool I wrote to help - but I'm really curious how other #Documentarians approach this challenge.
