It is approved! I have my first iOS app on the App Store!

If you are into video games, and happen to be playing Elden Ring, you might want to check it out. It’s an RPG play through tracker where you can keep track of NPCs, Locations and Quests in your game. Keep tabs on locations you’ve cleared (or haven’t), quests you’ve completed (or haven’t), notes about all of the above… I needed something more organized than a notebook.

(Bonus, this is also great for TTRPG tracking - I’ve started using it for two of my D&D games!)

https://shatteredring.com
