Well, crud.

Mere weeks after raving about Augment Code and signing up for a personal subscription because I was so happy with using it at work, they are changing their pricing model.

Under the plan I signed up for, I get “600 user messages” per month. I used about 200 of those during 3 days of my vacation. As a personal user, I could just about get away with 600 if I’m only using it on some nights and the occasional weekend day.

For their new plan, they are switching to a “credits” billing model. I’ve been informed that my messages over the last 7 days have  used about 1.5 times the number of credits of the average user on my plan. With the new model, my same monthly sub would translate to about 81 messages.

81.

Down from 600 that I was paying for when I signed up.

81, when I used ~200 in 3 days.

So basically a little over a day of usage will burn through my monthly subscription allotment.

I guess I’m in the market for a new agentic coding assistant for my personal projects!
