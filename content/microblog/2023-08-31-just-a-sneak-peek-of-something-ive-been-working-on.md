Just a sneak peek of something I've been working on...

Are you a #TechnicalWriter who works with engineers who occasionally forget to mention when something needs a documentation update?

Are you a #Developer or #DocsAsCode technical writer who makes or reviews PRs, or is assigned work in GitHub?

PR Focus is a macOS app that lets you watch GitHub repositories, see new and updated PRs in those repositories, and "Watch" or "Ignore" pull requests depending on whether they may need some action from you. PRs automatically show up in your "Reviewing" or "Assigned" columns if you're a requested reviewer or assignee, or if you become a reviewer. When the PR is merged, you can decide how long it should continue to show up in the dashboard before it moves to "Archived" or you can right-click to dismiss it from your dashboard.

Gone are the days when you had to check on multiple different repositories to see what's new and updated. No more losing track of outstanding PRs that require some action from you. Just one awesome app to help you stay on top of PRs you care about.
