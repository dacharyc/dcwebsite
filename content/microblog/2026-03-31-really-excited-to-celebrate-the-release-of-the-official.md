Really excited to celebrate the release of the official MongoDB Agent Skills 🎉

I had the opportunity to work with an excellent cross-functional team of collaborators to bring this initial collection of skills to your favorite coding agent.

This project has been a major proving ground for everything I've been posting about Skill development and quality. From day 1, we had CI running a structural and qualitative validator, we developed a custom review skill to help authors self-evaluate, and we had rigorous human review for technical accuracy, instructional quality, actionability, and consistency. Our contributors iterated through hundreds of review comments and multiple rounds of testing to produce this curated collection of skills.

Appreciative of all the work that all of our skill authors and contributors put in to bring you a high-quality collection! Now we get to move on to the next phase: figuring out how to operationalize contributions at this high quality bar on an ongoing basis 😁

If you give them a try, I'm curious to hear your feedback!
