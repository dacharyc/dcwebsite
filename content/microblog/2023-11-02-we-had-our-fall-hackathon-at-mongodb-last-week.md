We had our fall hackathon at MongoDB last week! I decided to use the time to get Google Analytics data for the documentation that my team writes *out* of an unwieldy spreadsheet and into MongoDB Atlas, where I can more easily analyze the data and build useful Charts visualizing the data for my team.

In Part 1 of my 3-part series about my hackathon project, I look at how to get data out of Google Sheets and into MongoDB Atlas using Google App Scripts, a Data API HTTPS Endpoint, and an Atlas Function.
