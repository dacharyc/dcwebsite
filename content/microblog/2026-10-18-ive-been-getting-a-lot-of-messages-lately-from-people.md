I've been getting a lot of messages lately from people asking me to post somewhere about their cool thing they have built on top of AFDocs. Rather than handling all of those as one-off requests, I've added a "Powered by AFDocs" page to the site!

If you have created a thing that uses AFDocs, and it's publicly available and attributes AFDocs, feel free to PR to add your thing to the page.

It's pretty neat to see a roundup of all the cool things people are building 🎉
