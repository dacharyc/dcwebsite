In a month of part-time work at my new #technicalwriting gig, I have spun up on their #software, crash-coursed on a couple of ancillary #technologies, consumed all of their existing documentation, built out a matrix for their #documentation redesign, and created a new version of their doc site.

The new doc site translates to 106 pages when printed as a PDF. Probably ~70-80 pages of that is new material. I'd estimate I have another 10-15 pages to draft, and then I might be able to reduce the volume of docs by tightening up the new organization a bit, and removing some stuff through content reuse.

Not bad for a month of part-time work!
