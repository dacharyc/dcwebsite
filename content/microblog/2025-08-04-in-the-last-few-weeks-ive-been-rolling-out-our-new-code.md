In the last few weeks, I've been rolling out our new code example test infrastructure for C#, Java, JavaScript, and Python. This week, I'm adding support for a shell tool. Next week, Go.

But all the tooling in the world does no good if people don't know how to use it.

I'm pairing this rollout with:

- High-level process documentation on our company wiki
- Implementation-specific READMEs with a consistent topic coverage for every programming language and product
- Workshops for writers who will be contributing net new code examples using our test infrastructure in upcoming projects
- Setting up a working group to onboard early adopters from every team across the docs org
- Providing a NotebookLM loaded with all of this source content so people can easily get answers without having to pour over all of the specifics

I will make updates to tooling and process based on these first few real-world projects and the feedback of our early adopters. And *then* I will socialize the rollout more broadly and seek to drive wider adoption.

I am super excited to bring this tooling to the wider org, and want to provide the training and resources to make it easy to adopt. 🎉 🙏

Technical writers - how do you roll out new tools and processes? Did I miss anything?
