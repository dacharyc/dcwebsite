Wow. Overnight, the tiny hobby app that I wrote to help me keep track of NPCs, Locations and Quests in Elden Ring reached #1 in its category on the App Store. It has been hovering between 1 and 2 ever since.

This is surreal. I wrote this app in two weekends and a handful of weeknights. I’ve been through tutorials and online courses but have had no formal CS education and don’t consider myself a “real developer.” Yet here we are. I’ve shipped an iOS app that people are buying.

This is what imposter syndrome looks like.
